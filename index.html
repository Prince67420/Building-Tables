Big Cat Information
Habitat Average Weight Lifespan
Lion Grasslands and savannas 190 kg 10–14 years

Big Cat Facts

  • Lions are the only big cats that live in social groups called prides.
  • Tigers are the largest species in the cat family.
  • Leopards are excellent climbers and often rest in trees.
